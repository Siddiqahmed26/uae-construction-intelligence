## 1. Macroeconomic & Tendering Context in the UAE Construction Market (2024–2026)

### 1.1 Global & Regional Construction Cost Positioning
According to **Turner & Townsend’s Global Construction Market Intelligence (GCMI) 2025 and 2026** surveys across six standard building typologies ****:
- **Dubai (2025 Baseline):** Ranked **74th most expensive globally** with an average construction cost of **USD 1,926/sq.m.** (**AED 7,073/sq.m.** | **AED 657.13/sq.ft.**).
- **Dubai (2026 Updated Baseline):** Reached an average construction cost of **USD 1,990/sq.m.** (**AED 7,308/sq.m.** | **AED 678.96/sq.ft.**), reflecting sustained upward pressure from specialist MEP capacity constraints and high-spec residential/hospitality pipelines.
- **Abu Dhabi (2025 Baseline):** Ranked **76th most expensive globally** with an average construction cost of **USD 1,872/sq.m.** (**AED 6,875/sq.m.** | **AED 638.70/sq.ft.**).
- **Regional GCC Comparison:** The UAE remains the most cost-efficient major construction hub in the GCC—approximately **38% to 40% less expensive** than **Riyadh, Saudi Arabia**, which ranked **37th globally** at **USD 3,112/sq.m.** (**AED 11,429/sq.m.** | **AED 1,061.77/sq.ft.**) due to acute giga-project resource competition in KSA.

### 1.2 UAE Project Pipeline & Output Velocity
- **Record Construction Output (Knight Frank H1 2025):** Total annual UAE construction output reached a record **USD 107.2 billion (AED 393.7 billion)** in 2024 and is projected to grow at **4.0% per annum** (a 22% cumulative expansion) to **USD 130.8 billion (AED 480.4 billion)** by 2029 ****.
- **Contract Awards (2020 – Aug 2025):** **USD 328.7 billion (AED 1.207 trillion)** in contracts were awarded across the UAE between 2020 and August 2025, with **Abu Dhabi** accounting for **USD 151.0 billion (46%)** and **Dubai** accounting for **USD 129.9 billion (39.5%)**—together representing **85% of national contract value** ****.
- **Active Pipeline Composition (JLL, Stonehaven & Knight Frank):** Active unawarded/underway UAE pipeline stands at **USD 590 billion to USD 772 billion** (~15% of the USD 3.9 trillion MENA pipeline), split into **USD 360 billion in Building Assets** and **USD 230 billion in Transportation & Infrastructure**. Within building construction, **Mixed-Use developments represent 39%–42%**, **Residential real estate 21%–28%**, **Data Centres 9%**, and **Hospitality 4%** ****.

### 2.2 Explicit Scope, Measurement Standards & Inclusions/Exclusions Matrix

As documented in **Stonehaven RICS 2025 (p. 10)**, **Turner & Townsend Tall Buildings Guide 2026**, and **Arcadis/Kolaboro Methodology Notes** ****, comparing two AED/sq.m. or AED/sq.ft. rates without normalizing their scope and area basis can introduce a **25% to 55% budgeting error**:

1. **Floor-Area Measurement Basis:**
   - **GFA (Gross Floor Area) / CFA (Construction Floor Area):** Measured to the external face of perimeter walls, **including** lift shafts, stairwells, lobbies, plant rooms, service risers, and covered balconies. Note that **Net Saleable Area (NSA) / Net Lettable Area (NLA)** is typically **70% to 82% of GFA/CFA** in high-rise towers; converting a GFA construction cost to a per-NSA basis increases the unit rate by **+22% to +43%**.
   - **BUA (Built-Up Area):** Includes all covered areas plus basements, podium parking, terraces, and mechanical floors.

2. **Standard Inclusions vs. Exclusions in UAE Consultant Benchmarks:**

| Cost Element / Scope Item | Shell & Core Benchmark (e.g., Commercial Office, Mall, T&T Tall Buildings) | Full Building Construction Benchmark (e.g., Residential Villa/Apt, Hotel Base) | Total Development Budget (Developer Feasibility Model) | Typical Allowance / Addition Over Direct Build Cost |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |

## 3. Market Benchmark Audit: *"~AED 250 per sq. ft. as a Possible Multiplier"*

### 3.1 Exact Mathematical Equivalence
$$\text{AED } 250.00\text{ / sq.ft.} \times 10.7639\text{ sq.ft./sq.m.} = \mathbf{AED\ 2,690.98\text{ / sq.m.}} \approx \mathbf{USD\ 732.74\text{ / sq.m.}}$$

### 3.2 Empirical Verification: Where AED 250/sq.ft. (~AED 2,691/sq.m.) Holds True vs. Where It Fails in 2025–2026

Our cross-consultant verification confirms that **AED 250/sq.ft. (~AED 2,691/sq.m.) is NOT a valid turnkey multiplier for standard residential, commercial, retail, or hospitality buildings in Dubai or Abu Dhabi in 2025–2026**. Instead, AED 250/sq.ft. represents a specific structural/industrial threshold or a legacy pre-pandemic (2019–2020) civil baseline:

#### A. Where ~AED 250/sq.ft. (~AED 2,691/sq.m.) HOLDS TRUE in 2025–2026:
1. **Industrial & Logistics Warehouses (Turnkey Shell to Mid-Spec) :**
   - Basic Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) steel warehouses in Dubai cost **AED 111–130/sq.ft.** (AED 1,200–1,400/m²), and in Sharjah/Ajman/RAK cost **AED 88–111/sq.ft.** (AED 950–1,200/m²).
   - Standard RCC/hybrid warehouses with basic firefighting and offices cost **AED 130–204/sq.ft.** (AED 1,400–2,200/m²).
   - Institutional light industrial units (Stonehaven RICS 2025) start at **AED 279/sq.ft.** (AED 3,000/m²). Thus, **AED 250/sq.ft.** accurately buys a robust, mid-to-high-spec industrial warehouse or light factory shell with basic MEP.
2. **Multi-Storey Above-Ground Car Parking Structures :**
   - Stonehaven RICS 2025 benchmarks multi-storey car parks at **AED 2,500–3,600/m² (AED 232–334/sq.ft.)**. Thus, **AED 250/sq.ft.** sits squarely within the standard multi-storey parking structure band.
3. **"Grey Structure / Skeleton Only" (Shell & Core Civil Package) for Villas & Low-Rise Buildings :**
   - In a standard UAE residential villa or G+4 building, the civil/structural skeleton (excavation, substructure, concrete frame, rebar, and blockwork—representing ~35%–42% of total turnkey cost) costs **AED 160–250/sq.ft. (AED 1,720–2,690/m²)** before MEP, façade glazing, flooring, joinery, and external works. Many private villa owners and small developers in the UAE mistakenly cite the **civil skeleton contract rate (~AED 220–250/sq.ft.)** as the "construction cost," omitting the subsequent MEP and fit-out packages.
4. **Economy Townhouses / Basic Low-Rise Villas in Peripheral Northern Emirates (Ajman / Umm Al Quwain) or Historical 2019–2020 Rates [Derived]:**
   - During the 2019–2020 market trough (when Emirates Steel rebar dropped below AED 1,850/tonne and C40 concrete was ~AED 175/m³), high-volume developer townhouses in outer Dubailand, Sharjah, and Ajman were tendered at **AED 240–275/sq.ft. (AED 2,580–2,960/m²)**.
   - By 2025–2026, even the most basic economy villa in the UAE starts at **AED 297–350/sq.ft. (AED 3,200–3,767/m²)** in Ajman/outer zones and **AED 372–390/sq.ft. (AED 4,000–4,200/m²)** in Dubai/Abu Dhabi.

## 6. Synthesis & Practical Cost-Estimation Rules for UAE Feasibility Modeling

1. **Never Apply a Single Emirate-Wide Multiplier:** Direct construction costs in the UAE span a **26x range** from **AED 1,200/m² (AED 111/sq.ft.)** for a basic PEB warehouse to **AED 31,000/m² (AED 2,880/sq.ft.)** for a super-luxury custom mansion, and up to **AED 52,150/m² (AED 4,845/sq.ft.)** for a large hyperscale data centre ****.
2. **Geographic Cost Hierarchy Across the 7 Emirates:**
   - **Tier 1 (Highest):** **Dubai** (Index = 100; 2025 GCMI avg USD 1,926/m², 2026 avg USD 1,990/m²) and **Abu Dhabi** (Index = 97.2; 2025 GCMI avg USD 1,872/m²). Note that **Ras Al Khaimah waterfront/Al Marjan Island** hospitality and branded residential developments now price within **92%–100% of Dubai rates** due to contractor mobilization and marine foundation premiums.
   - **Tier 2 (Moderate — -8% to -15% vs. Dubai):** **Sharjah** (except luxury insulated villas which match Dubai) and **inland Ras Al Khaimah / Fujairah**.
   - **Tier 3 (Most Cost-Effective — -18% to -28% vs. Dubai):** **Ajman** and **Umm Al Quwain**, driven by lower contractor overheads, faster municipal approvals, and lower prelims.
3. **From Base Construction Cost to Total Development Cost (Soft-Cost Multiplier):**
   - To move from a consultant's **GFA Construction Cost Benchmark** to a **Total Development Cost (Excl. Land & Finance)**, add:
     - **+3% to +8%** for External Works & Landscaping (if excluded from building rate)
     - **+5% to +10%** for Statutory Authority Approvals, Building Permits & DEWA/TAQA/District Cooling Connection Charges
     - **+8% to +12%** for Professional Consultant Fees (Architecture, Engineering, QS, PM, Supervision)
     - **+5% to +10%** for Design & Construction Contingency
     - **+3.3% to +5.0% per annum** for Tender Price Escalation to midpoint of construction
     - **+5.0% UAE VAT** on all taxable construction and professional services.
   - Consequently, **Total All-In CapEx (excluding land and developer finance)** is typically **1.25x to 1.38x** the baseline construction cost per square meter.
