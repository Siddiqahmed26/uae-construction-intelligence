### 3.2 Granular Year-by-Year Breakdown: Dubai & Abu Dhabi (2025–2029)

#### A. Dubai Residential & Office Pipeline (2025–2029)
- **2025:**
  - *Scheduled Residential:* ~82,000 units | *Actual/Realistic Completions (ValuStrat / Property Monitor):* **37,000 – 40,400 units** (~49%–55% realization rate).
  - *Asset Split:* ~83% Apartments (~32,000 units) / ~17% Villas & Townhouses (~6,800 units).
- **2026:**
  - *Scheduled Residential:* ~71,000 – 91,000 units | *Realistic Forecast (Cushman & Wakefield / ValuStrat):* **34,000 – 48,000 units**.
  - *Office GLA Deliveries:* **~1.7 million sq.ft.** (including Innovation Two in DIFC, Sweid One in JLT, Technohub 5 in DSO).
- **2027 (Peak Delivery Stress-Test Window):**
  - *Scheduled Residential:* **90,000 – 120,000+ units** (as 2023–2024 off-plan launch waves and 2025–2026 spillovers converge) | *Realistic Forecast:* **50,000 – 65,000 units**.
  - *Office GLA Deliveries:* **~3.2 million sq.ft.** (Immersive Tower DIFC, Business Bay strata towers, Expo City commercial).
- **2028–2029:**
  - *Scheduled Residential:* **110,000 – 140,000 units combined** across 2028–2029 (anchored by Emaar's The Oasis/Heights, Palm Jebel Ali, Azizi Venice, Damac Islands, Sobha Hartland II).
  - *Office GLA Deliveries (2028–2030):* **~10.9 million sq.ft.**, led by **DIFC Zabeel District expansion** (adding 4M–7M sq.ft. of Grade-A office space) and Sheikh Zayed Road / Business Bay (**8.3M sq.ft.** cumulative pipeline).

#### B. Abu Dhabi Residential & Office Pipeline (2025–2029)
- **2025:**
  - *Residential Completions:* **~4,500 – 5,500 units** (constrained supply supporting double-digit capital appreciation).
  - *Office NLA/GLA Deliveries:* **~90,000 sq.m.** (~968,000 sq.ft.).
- **2026:**
  - *Residential Completions:* **~5,000 – 6,500 units** (led by Aldar handovers on Yas Island, Saadiyat Lagoons Phase 1, and Al Reem Island).
  - *Office NLA/GLA Deliveries:* **~75,000 sq.m.** (~807,000 sq.ft., concentrated in Masdar City Square MC2 and The Link).
- **2027:**
  - *Scheduled Residential Surge (ADREC / Cushman & Wakefield):* **~18,900 units** scheduled as major 2023–2024 island launches reach completion.
  - *Office NLA/GLA Deliveries:* **~36,000 sq.m.** (~387,000 sq.ft. — acute Grade-A shortage prior to 2028 wave).
- **2028–2029:**
  - *Scheduled Residential Peak:* **~21,800 units in 2028** and **~15,000+ units in 2029** (driven by Hudayriyat Island, Fahid Island, Ramhan Island, Saadiyat Lagoons, and Balghaiylam). Over **70% of all new supply** is concentrated on Yas, Saadiyat, Reem, Hudayriyat, and Fahid islands.
  - *Office NLA/GLA Deliveries (2028–2030):* **~364,000 sq.m.** (~3.92 million sq.ft.), peaking in 2029 with **One Maryah Place** (98,000 sq.m. Grade-A office in ADGM) and Saadiyat Grove commercial spaces.
