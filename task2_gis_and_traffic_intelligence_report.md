## 2. Work Package & Milestone Completion Summary

### Work Package A — Research & Data Collection (Milestone 1)
1. **A1 — Dubai & Sharjah Open-Data & Transport Source Register (`15_transport_open_data_register`):** Documented `18` official open-data and transport datasets across Dubai Pulse (`data.dubai.gov.ae`), RTA Open Data, Dubai Land Department (`dld_projects-open`), Dubai Municipality GIS (`gis.dm.gov.ae` / Makani), Salik PJSC, Parkin PJSC, DEWA EV Green Charger, Sharjah Roads & Transport Authority (`srta.gov.ae`), Sharjah Directorate of Town Planning & Survey (`sdtps.gov.ae`), Bayanat.ae, and OpenStreetMap Overpass, recording all 16 required fields per dataset (publishing authority, coverage, format, update frequency, API availability, access restrictions, licensing, CRS, historical availability, prototype suitability, URL, and verification date).
2. **A2 — Road-Network & Capacity Dataset (`16_road_network_dataset`):** Mapped `20` primary Dubai & Sharjah road corridors (`16` Dubai: `E11`, `E311`, `E44` North & South, `E611`, `D61` Hessa West & East, `D63` Umm Suqeim, `D72` Al Quoz, `D71` Financial Centre Rd, `D68` Al Wasl, `D94` King Salman St, `D89` Airport Rd, `E66`, `E77`, and JVC Circle Blvd; `4` Sharjah: `E311`, `E611`, `E88` Al Dhaid Rd, and `E11` Al Ittihad Rd).
   - **Strict Capacity Guardrail:** Published RTA statutory corridor capacities (e.g., Hessa St `4,000 -> 8,000 vph/dir`; Al Khail Rd `+15,000 vph`; Umm Suqeim `12,000 vph` both directions; Ras Al Khor `10,000 vph/dir`) are explicitly separated from `HCM_7TH_EDITION_ESTIMATE` engineering values (`1,900–2,000 pc/h/ln` freeways; `800–950 veh/h/ln` signalized arterials).
3. **A3 — Traffic & Congestion Observations (`17_traffic_congestion_observations`):** Compiled `18` multi-period (`Morning Peak 07:30–09:30`, `Midday 12:00–14:00`, `Evening Peak 17:00–20:00`, `Night 22:00–05:00`) and historical (`2024 vs. 2026`) observations, explicitly distinguishing `Observed` telemetry/benchmarks from `Modelled Estimate` off-peak profiles.
4. **A4 — TomTom & Traffic Provider Evaluation (`18_tomtom_and_provider_assessment`):** Evaluated `6` providers (`TomTom Traffic Flow & Incidents API`, `TomTom Move Historical Portal`, `Dubai Pulse & RTA Open Data`, `HERE Traffic API v7`, `Google Maps Platform Routes API`, and `Mapbox Traffic / MapLibre + OSM`).
   - **Verdict:** Recommended a **Hybrid Architecture**: MapLibre/Leaflet + OpenStreetMap geometry + Dubai Pulse/RTA open baseline + **TomTom Traffic Flow/Incidents API** (`2,500 free daily requests/day`, 30–60s updates, permissive map overlay terms) for live tile overlays, reserving **TomTom Move** for commercial historical time-series procurement.
5. **A5 — Construction Project Enhancement (`04_project_inventory` + `21d_jvc_and_community_subdevelopments`):** Deduplicated all 20 Dubai flagship project coordinates to exact plot-level WGS84 centroids and added `5` multi-tower JVC sub-development clusters (`SUB-JVC-01..05` referencing Task 1 `DEV-05` Binghatti, `DEV-14` Ellington, `DEV-15` Samana, `DEV-25` Tiger Properties, and `DEV-28` Nakheel) without duplicating or conflicting with Task 1's 85-project inventory.
6. **A6, A7 & A8 — Traffic Impact Studies, HGV Logistics & Mobility Facilities (`19_traffic_impact_studies_and_logistics` & `20_parking_and_mobility_facilities`):** Documented `8` corridor & masterplan TIS records (clarifying why plot-level consultant TIS submissions in RTA's TES portal are confidential while RTA/SRTA corridor studies are public), Dubai's statutory **HGV Truck Ban schedules** (`06:00–22:00` on `E11`; 3-peak bans on `E311`/`E44`/`D61`; `24/7 exempt` on `E611`), **Salik dynamic tolling** (`AED 6` peak / `AED 4` off-peak / `AED 0` night effective Jan 31, 2025), **Parkin PJSC** (`207,000+` spaces including `29,600` in JVC/DMC), **DEWA EV Green Chargers** (`1,270+` points), and **RTA/Talabat dark-kitchen delivery-bike hubs**.

### Work Packages B, C, D, E & F — Spatial Database, 11-Layer Interactive Map, 3 Use Cases & Deterministic Q&A (Milestones 2–6)

1. **4-Tier Epistemic Impact Classification (`22_project_road_impact_links` — `37` Links):**
   - **Tier 1 (`Observed Traffic Congestion`):** Direct RTA/TomTom speed and travel-time degradation on the corridor.
   - **Tier 2 (`Spatial Association`):** Active construction project located within `500m–1.5km` of a congested corridor (`geodesic distance` computed in WGS84/UTM Zone 40N), explicitly stating that proximity alone does not prove causation.
   - **Tier 3 (`Modelled Construction Impact`):** Engineering estimate of construction HGV concrete/rebar deliveries (derived from Task 1 concrete $\text{m}^3$ and steel tonnage divided by structural duration and `8 m³` mixer / `25t` trailer loads) constrained to RTA's legal `Midday (08:30–13:00)` and `Night (20:30–05:00)` windows, plus post-handover resident trip generation (`RTA DTGPRM v2.0`).
   - **Tier 4 (`Confirmed Causal Impact`):** Assigned **strictly** where official RTA/SRTA roadwork notices confirm active work-zone lane constrictions or capacity upgrades (`PRJ-D8` Dubai Metro Blue Line stations on E66/D89; RTA AED 689M Hessa St; AED 670M Ras Al Khor; SRTA Al Dhaid Bridge).
2. **Why High Construction GFA $\neq$ Severe Traffic Congestion:**
   - **Dubai South / DWC (`COM-DXB-04`)** has the largest active structural GFA in the UAE (`7.85M m²`), yet experiences **Low (`86 km/h` on `E611`, LOS B)** congestion because `Emirates Road (E611)` is a 12-lane (`11,400 vph/dir`) freight freeway **100% exempt from daytime HGV bans**.
   - **Jumeirah Village Circle (`COM-DXB-01`, `3.42M m²` GFA, `38` active developments)** and **Business Bay / Downtown (`COM-DXB-03`, `3.85M m²` GFA)** experience **Severe (`25–34 km/h`, LOS E–F)** peak congestion because resident commuter waves and delivery motorcycles funnel through signalized collector gates (`D61` Hessa St and `D71` Financial Centre Rd), while HGV concrete mixers (`~168–280 trips/hr` in JVC) are compressed into the `Midday` and `Night` windows.
